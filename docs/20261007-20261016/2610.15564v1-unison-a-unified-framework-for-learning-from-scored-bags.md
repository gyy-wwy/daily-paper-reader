## 摘要
我们提出 UNISON，一个统一框架，它将多示例学习与推荐系统联系起来，将二者都视为从由（物品，分数）对构成的带评分包集合中学习的问题。与传统方法将包级分类和个性化推荐视为独立问题不同，UNISON 将学习分解为三个相互关联的阶段：(1) 学习通用物品表示，(2) 通过轻量级个性化参数对包特有的偏好模式进行建模，(3) 从学到的偏好中推断包级特征。我们通过一阶分析论证了采用包特定线性评分这一架构选择的合理性：当物品嵌入作为分数的函数足够平滑时，线性函数可以近似该分数，其误差由底层偏好函数的曲率所控制的泰勒余项界定。针对冷启动场景，我们证明冷包拟合问题可约化为不带岭正则的线性回归，并具有闭式解；同时为迭代变体提供收敛性保证。在实证上，UNISON 在多个领域中达到或超越最先进方法：娱乐（图书、电影）、免疫学（基于 T 细胞受体（TCR）的分类）和微生物组，并具有更优的计算效率和可解释性。一项受控合成实验进一步证实，即使投影 $φ$ 无法访问原始评分机制，包特定参数（$w_j, b_j$）仍能恢复驱动物品分数的潜在因子（AUC > 0.998）。UNISON 可在 https://github.com/louzounlab/UNISON 获取。

## Abstract
We introduce UNISON, a unified framework that bridges multiple instance learning and recommendation systems by treating both as instances of learning from scored bag collections of (item, score) pairs. Unlike traditional approaches that address bag-level classification and personalized recommendation as separate problems, UNISON decomposes learning into three interconnected stages: (1) learning universal item representations, (2) modeling bag-specific preference patterns through lightweight personalized parameters, and (3) inferring bag-level characteristics from learned preferences. We justify the architectural choice of linear bag-specific scoring through a first-order analysis: when item embeddings are sufficiently smooth in the score, a linear function approximates the score up to a Taylor remainder controlled by the curvature of the underlying preference function. For cold-start scenarios, we show that the cold-bag fitting problem reduces to ridge-less linear regression with a closed-form solution, and provide convergence guarantees for the iterative variant. Empirically, UNISON matches or outperforms state-of-the-art approaches across diverse domains: entertainment (books, movies), immunology (T cell receptor (TCR) based classification), and microbiome, offering superior computational efficiency and interpretability. A controlled synthetic experiment further confirms that the bag-specific parameters ($w_j, b_j$) recover the latent factors that drive item scores even when the projection $φ$ has no access to the original scoring mechanism (AUC > 0.998). UNISON is available at https://github.com/louzounlab/UNISON.
